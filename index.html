About Me

Nkemdi Patrick Onwordi
Digital Product Manager & Senior Product/Solution Owner

Nkemdi Patrick Onwordi is a seasoned Digital Product Manager and Senior Product/Solution Owner with over a decade of experience delivering high-impact technology solutions across ICT, energy, and public-sector environments. With an M.Sc in Electrical Engineering and advanced Agile certifications (PSPO, SAFe 6.0 PO/PM, CSPO), he blends technical expertise with strategic product leadership and user-centred design.

At OFGEM, Nkemdi leads the delivery of user-focused digital services, driving API integrations, data-driven decision-making, risk assessment, and stakeholder engagement within complex regulatory landscapes. His career spans connectivity, software development, Wi-Fi, and IT advisory, shaped significantly by hands-on roles at Level 4 IT Solutions and ipNX Nigeria Limited, where he built strong foundations in technical service delivery, customer satisfaction, and operational excellence.

Nkemdi is also deeply passionate about youth development. His work with young people centres on career guidance, mentorship, coaching, and offering practical support that empowers them to thrive and reach their full potential.

A thoughtful writer and product innovator, he explores themes of kindness, equity, dignity of labour, and societal growth. He is currently developing several digital products—including a ride-and-share marketplace and intelligent field-data collection tools—designed for real-world impact, security, and long-term scalability.
